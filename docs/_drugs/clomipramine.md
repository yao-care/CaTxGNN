## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, clomipramine is a potent serotonin reuptake inhibitor, and its metabolite desmethylclomipramine adds noradrenergic activity. This serotonergic and noradrenergic modulation underlies efficacy across anxiety-spectrum conditions.

OCD has long been grouped with anxiety disorders, and the literature describes it as sharing features of both anxiety and affective disorders. Clomipramine's efficacy in OCD is well established. For panic disorder and agoraphobia, support comes from network meta-analyses, guidelines, and older controlled studies, in which clomipramine served as a reference drug.

**Caveat on the evidence level:** The Phase 3 trials listed below are mostly OCD augmentation or pediatric OCD studies, and clomipramine is not their test drug. The L1 label reflects the formal rules, but evidence for anxiety disorders themselves is weaker than the label suggests.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** epilepsy, manic bipolar affective disorder, liver disease, gallbladder disease, substance abuse/dependence, malignant hyperthermia, phaeochromocytoma and related adrenal or sympathetic tumours, glaucoma, hyperthyroidism (including Graves disease and thyrotoxicosis), neuroblastoma, and several cancers (duodenum, small intestine, heart, adrenal gland).
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. Major-level examples include epinephrine, bupropion, lorcaserin, potassium citrate, and cisapride. Moderate-level examples include famotidine, cimetidine, clarithromycin, morphine, acetylsalicylic acid, and several anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium).
- **Other signals from the literature:** seizure risk, anticholinergic and cardiac effects, overdose toxicity typical of tricyclics, and a rare case report of pancytopenia.

Please also refer to the package insert, as label warnings were not available for this report.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Clomipramine is already marketed in Canada, and its serotonergic mechanism is well established in OCD. Support for panic disorder and agoraphobia comes from meta-analyses, guidelines, and small older studies. Registered trials directly testing clomipramine in anxiety disorders are lacking, and the safety burden is substantial, so guardrails are needed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Health Canada
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Controlled trials of clomipramine in a defined anxiety disorder (such as panic disorder), rather than OCD
- A safety plan covering cardiac monitoring (ECG), seizure risk, anticholinergic effects, overdose risk, and the high drug interaction burden
