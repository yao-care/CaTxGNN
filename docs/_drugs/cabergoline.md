## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for cabergoline: "Cabergoline is an ergot derivative and dopamine receptor agonist with a high affinity for D2 receptors. Results of in vitro studies demonstrate that cabergoline exerts a direct inhibitory effect on the secretion of prolactin by rat pituitary lactotrophs." ([DailyMed: cabergoline prescribing information, Mylan Pharmaceuticals Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=e497366b-a124-4d7f-bd45-a883c392d4bb), label version 2026-06-30; accessed 2026-11-19.) Cabergoline is generally known as a dopamine D2 receptor agonist.

No mechanistic rationale connecting dopamine D2 agonism to progeria-short stature-pigmented nevi syndrome could be identified from the available data. The high TxGNN score (0.9906) is a computational model output and is not supported by any trial or publication. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
