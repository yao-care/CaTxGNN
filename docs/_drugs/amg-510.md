## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology data indicate that AMG-510 is a covalent inhibitor of KRAS G12C. In a cell-based assay it blocks downstream ERK1/2 phosphorylation with an IC50 of 68 nM. Its use in KRAS G12C-mutated NSCLC is described in the pharmacology data, which note FDA approval in May 2021 for patients who had received at least one prior systemic therapy.

Transitional cell carcinoma (urothelial carcinoma) is a different tumour type from NSCLC. The drug would be relevant only for the small subset of urothelial tumours carrying a KRAS G12C mutation. KRAS mutations in urothelial carcinoma are mostly not G12C. The mechanistic link is therefore plausible only for a small, biomarker-defined group, and the compiled data give no support for it.

Other high-ranking predictions are also urothelial subtypes (prostatic urethra, renal pelvis, bladder sarcomatoid variants) and share the same limitation. Papillary urothelial tumours are more often driven by FGFR3 and HRAS alterations than by KRAS G12C. Some lower-ranked predictions have no plausible link at all. Esotropia, a non-cancer eye movement disorder, is most likely a model artifact and probable false positive.
