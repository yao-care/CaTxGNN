**To proceed, the following is needed:**

- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank

- A targeted literature search on autism-related irritability before any upgrade of the Asperger syndrome candidate
- For major affective disorder: separate evaluation of the depressive and manic indications, and a safety monitoring plan for metabolic, prolactin and extrapyramidal effects
