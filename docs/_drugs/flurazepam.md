## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Flurazepam is a benzodiazepine. Its class acts as a positive allosteric modulator of GABA-A receptors, which enhances inhibitory neurotransmission and promotes sleep onset and maintenance. A 2023 cryo-EM study of native GABA-A receptor assemblies (PMID 37730991) describes these receptors as the target of sedatives and hypnotics, which is consistent with this mechanism.

The predicted indication is the classic use of this drug. Reviews in the evidence set describe flurazepam as the first benzodiazepine hypnotic, available from 1970. One review reports that it is effective for both sleep induction and maintenance and keeps most of its efficacy over 4 weeks of nightly use. The very high TxGNN score therefore reflects an established link.

Because the original indication field is empty, this is probably not a true repurposing case. The label indication should be checked against the regulatory record before the drug is treated as a repurposing candidate.
