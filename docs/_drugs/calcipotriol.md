## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, calcipotriol is a vitamin D3 analog that acts through the vitamin D receptor (VDR). It inhibits keratinocyte proliferation, promotes differentiation, and induces apoptosis.

Psoriasis and seborrheic keratosis are both skin conditions with abnormal keratinocyte growth. Seborrheic keratosis is a common benign epidermal tumour of older adults. The effects above are therefore plausibly relevant to it. Published reports suggest that apoptosis induction may be the mechanism in senile warts (an older term for seborrheic keratosis).

The evidence is limited to small clinical studies and case reports. The mechanistic link is a hypothesis and has not been confirmed in controlled trials.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold (research question)**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high, and the biological rationale is plausible. The supporting evidence is only small, mostly uncontrolled clinical reports, with no registered trials and no comparative data against standard treatments (cryotherapy, curettage, laser). Safety label data is also missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert warnings, contraindications, and approved indications (currently blocking)
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA)
- A controlled study (ideally randomized) in seborrheic keratosis, compared with standard destructive therapies
- Tolerability and local-irritation assessment on facial skin, and calcium monitoring if used over larger areas
- Review of the knowledge-graph contraindication signals and interaction severity against the official label

**Other predicted indications (ranks 2–10):** All carry weak or no direct evidence and are on Hold. Vulvitis and vulvar neoplasm have only indirect or case-level support (via vulvar psoriasis and benign genital acanthomas, respectively). The rest (vulvar inverted follicular keratosis, vulvovaginitis, vulvar ulceration, vulvar squamous neoplasm, postmenopausal atrophic vaginitis, bone Paget disease, breast fibrocystic disease) are computational predictions only, and several raise mucosal-irritation or hypercalcemia concerns.
