## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. From what is known, benralizumab targets the IL-5 receptor alpha subunit (IL5RA) and depletes eosinophils and basophils through antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC).

The link to immune thrombocytopenia is speculative. Eosinophil-driven inflammation is not an established driver of platelet destruction, which is mainly mediated by autoantibodies and related immune mechanisms. The high score reflects a model-based association rather than a demonstrated biological or clinical rationale.
