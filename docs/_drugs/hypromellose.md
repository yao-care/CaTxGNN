## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Hypromellose is a largely inert cellulose-ether excipient and ocular lubricant with negligible systemic absorption. Its effect in eye drops is physical: it increases viscosity and helps retain moisture on the ocular surface.

The predicted disease is a rare inherited disorder involving liver vascular obstruction and immune deficiency. Hypromellose has no known effect on endothelial injury, sinusoidal obstruction or immune signalling. **No plausible mechanistic link was identified.** The 98.30% score is a graph-based output only. It has no clinical or preclinical support and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.
