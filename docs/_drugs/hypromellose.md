## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Hypromellose is an inert cellulose-derived material used as a viscosity agent, film-coating agent and ophthalmic lubricant. It is negligibly absorbed into the bloodstream.

The predicted disease is a rare genetic immunodeficiency caused by loss of function of the SP110 gene, with liver vascular injury. No biological link was identified between hypromellose and this disease. The high score (98.30%) appears to reflect patterns in the knowledge graph rather than real pharmacological activity.

The other top predictions fall into the same pattern: rare genetic immune or developmental syndromes (for example, pancytopenia due to IKZF1 mutations, CRAC channel-related immunodeficiency, immuno-osseous dysplasia), hepatic veno-occlusive disease, and HHV-8-related tumours. None has a plausible mechanism. Psoriasis (rank 8) is the only one with a conceivable role, and only as a topical vehicle or emollient-like base, not as a treatment acting on the disease itself.
