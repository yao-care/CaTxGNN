## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for periciazine. Based on general pharmacology, periciazine is a phenothiazine antipsychotic. Other drugs in this class, such as chlorpromazine, block dopamine D2 receptors and are used for acute migraine and migraine-associated nausea.

The reasoning is therefore at the class level and indirect. It is not supported by periciazine-specific data. The very high TxGNN score reflects a computational prediction only and is not clinical evidence.

Other predicted candidates (migraine with brainstem aura, dysthymic disorder, and several rare skin disorders) are weaker or have no identifiable mechanistic link. Migraine disorder is the most plausible of them.
