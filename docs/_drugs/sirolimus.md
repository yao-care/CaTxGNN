## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Sirolimus is known to inhibit mTOR, a kinase that sits downstream of the PI3K/AKT pathway and drives cell growth and survival.

This pathway is frequently activated in soft tissue sarcomas, including liposarcoma. Examples include PTEN loss and tumours with MDM2/CDK4 amplification. Blocking mTOR could therefore slow tumour growth, and mouse models cited in the trial description support this idea.

A completed Phase 2 trial tested sirolimus plus cyclophosphamide in myxoid liposarcoma and chondrosarcoma. Its single-arm design and mixed sarcoma population limit what can be concluded for liposarcoma specifically.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions are recorded. The 20 reviewed include these.
  - **Major**: amphotericin B (including the lipid complex), mesalazine, balsalazide and clarithromycin.
  - **Moderate**: corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, triamcinolone), many glucose-lowering agents (metformin, pioglitazone, acarbose, alogliptin, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, chlorpropamide, albiglutide), cimetidine and aprepitant.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only directly relevant study is a single-arm Phase 2 trial of a sirolimus combination in a mixed sarcoma population. There is no randomized or liposarcoma-specific efficacy data. Package insert warnings and contraindications are also missing, so safety screening cannot proceed yet.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Liposarcoma-specific results from NCT02821507, or a randomized trial in liposarcoma
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Health Canada
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- A safety and interaction-monitoring plan, particularly for combination use with cyclophosphamide
