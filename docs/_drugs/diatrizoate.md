## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Diatrizoate is an iodinated, high-osmolar contrast agent. It has no known immunomodulatory or anti-inflammatory activity, and no therapeutic mechanism links it to rheumatoid arthritis.

The high TxGNN score (0.9902) is a model output only. Neither clinical nor mechanistic evidence supports it. The one linked publication (PMID 6491310, 1984) describes separating blood cells from normal and rheumatoid arthritis patients by density-gradient centrifugation. The medium used was Ficoll-Hypaque, which contains diatrizoate salts. Here diatrizoate is a laboratory separation reagent and was not given to patients. The prediction may reflect this co-occurrence in the literature rather than a real treatment effect, so it should be treated with caution.
