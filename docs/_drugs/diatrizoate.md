## Safety Considerations

- **Sickle cell disease signal (from the literature for a related prediction, hemoglobinopathy):** Reports describe hyperosmolar diatrizoate contrast media inducing erythrocyte sickling and hemolysis in patients with sickle cell disease and HbSC disease. These include acute hemolysis, post-angiographic blindness and a fatality. This is a safety signal, not a repurposing opportunity.

For other safety information, please refer to the package insert.
