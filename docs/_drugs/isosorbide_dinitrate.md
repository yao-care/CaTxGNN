## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on general pharmacology, ISDN is an organic nitrate that releases nitric oxide, raising cGMP in smooth muscle and causing vasodilation.

For alopecia, one speculative hypothesis is that vasodilation could improve scalp perfusion around hair follicles, a mechanism shared with other vasodilators. No clinical trial or publication was found to support this, so it should be treated as a hypothesis only. The other hair-related predictions (congenital hypotrichosis milia, hypotrichosis simplex of the scalp, diffuse alopecia areata, hypertrichosis) are likewise prediction-only. For several of them, such as genetic hair disorders and autoimmune alopecia areata, the vasodilatory mechanism does not address the underlying cause.

### Secondary finding: pulmonary hypertension

Among the other predictions, pulmonary hypertension (score 0.9998) has the most literature. Vasodilation by ISDN lowers pulmonary artery pressure and preload, which is mechanistically coherent. The retrieved studies are mostly small haemodynamic studies in COPD-associated pulmonary hypertension, end-stage cardiac disease and other settings, plus animal work. No registered trials were found. Reported concerns include possible ventilation/perfusion mismatch and a lack of long-term benefit data. For this disease the evidence level would be L3, but it is not the headline prediction of this report.

## Safety Considerations

- **Relations recorded in the knowledge graph (not label text):** The TxGNN knowledge graph records contraindication relations with conditions including hypotensive disorder, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, stroke and cerebrovascular disorders, intracerebral hemorrhage, intracranial hypertension, anaemias and hemoglobinopathy, and hyperthyroidism/Graves disease. These come from the graph, not from product labelling, and should be checked against the package insert.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found in the DDI database. Examples rated Moderate include bupropion, morphine, opium, dronabinol, nabilone, sapropterin, rosiglitazone, and the SGLT2 inhibitors canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin and ertugliflozin. Omeprazole is rated Minor.
- **Other cautions from the literature:** Nitrate tolerance develops quickly with continued use, and hypotension is a recognised risk of vasodilation, particularly in dialysis patients and those with low blood pressure.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
