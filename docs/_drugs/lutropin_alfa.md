## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Lutropin alfa is recombinant LH and acts on the LH/choriogonadotropin receptor (LHCGR) in gonadal tissue.

We could not identify a plausible direct link between this mechanism and POTS. POTS is an autonomic disorder. Any connection would be speculative, for example through sex-hormone modulation of autonomic tone.

The high TxGNN score reflects a knowledge-graph prediction and has not been backed by biological or clinical evidence. The other nine top-ranked predictions have the same problem (esophageal and duodenal conditions, Tourette syndrome, trichotillomania, Raynaud disease, sinoatrial block, and others). None has a supporting mechanism. This suggests the predictions reflect graph topology rather than biology.
