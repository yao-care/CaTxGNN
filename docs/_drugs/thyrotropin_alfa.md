## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The migraine prediction rests on the model score alone. There are no trials, no relevant publications, and no known mechanism linking TSH receptor agonism to migraine. Evidence level is L5.

One other prediction in the Evidence Pack has some support. For **hyperthyroidism** (rank 10, L4, "Research Question"), two completed Phase 2 trials exist: NCT00145366 (n=110) and NCT00454220 (n=96). Both studied rhTSH given with radioiodine in goiter, so the match to hyperthyroidism is indirect. TSH stimulation can also cause transient thyrotoxicosis, so safety would be the main concern.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data (MOA) to allow a mechanistic-link analysis
- Health Canada package insert warnings and contraindications, which block safety screening
- Approved indication text and dosage form for the Canadian license
- A systematic literature search specific to thyrotropin alfa and migraine, since none has been retrieved
- For hyperthyroidism, full-text review of the two Phase 2 trials to confirm design and population, which could raise the evidence level to L2
