## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, dexpanthenol is converted in the body to pantothenic acid, a precursor of coenzyme A. Coenzyme A supports epithelial proliferation and mucosal repair, so a role in mucosal healing is conceivable.

Anorectal stricture is a narrowing of the anorectal canal, usually caused by fibrosis and scarring. This is a structural problem, and repairing the epithelium is not the same as reversing fibrotic narrowing. No mechanistic or clinical data support a benefit in this condition, so the high score should be read as a hypothesis to test, not as evidence.

Other predictions for this drug are also low-confidence. Six of the seven listed have no trials or literature at all. The exception is exanthem (skin eruption), which has indirect dermatological trials only, and it is not the primary prediction evaluated here.
