## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, valine is a BCAA supplied as part of amino acid nutrition formulations. Amino acid metabolism is altered in cholestatic liver disease, so a metabolic link to sclerosing cholangitis is plausible.

The two retrieved papers are observational or genetic-epidemiology studies. A 2005 cohort study related plasma tyrosine to fatigue in primary biliary cirrhosis and primary sclerosing cholangitis. A 2024 Mendelian randomization study linked blood metabolites and metabolic pathways to cholestatic liver disease. Neither tests valine as an intervention. These are hypothesis-generating signals only, and the high TxGNN score is a model prediction, not clinical evidence.
