# Vinblastine: From Its Existing Cancer Use to Rhabdomyosarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Vinblastine is a vinca alkaloid chemotherapy drug that is currently marketed in Canada. The Canadian licence records provided do not state its approved indication.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Rhabdomyosarcoma**, but there are **0 registered clinical trials** and **15 publications** for this indication.
Most of the publications are case reports, studies of the related drug vinorelbine, or preclinical work, so the evidence is weak.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Vinblastine binds tubulin, blocks microtubule polymerization and causes mitotic arrest. Cells that divide rapidly are the most affected. Rhabdomyosarcoma is a fast-growing pediatric soft tissue sarcoma, so this mechanism is plausible for it.

Two preclinical papers support the link. One is a mouse xenograft study of vinca alkaloid selectivity in human rhabdomyosarcoma (PMID 3329524). The other shows microtubule-destabilizing drugs acting synergistically with PLK1 inhibitors in rhabdomyosarcoma models (PMID 26024389).

The clinical data are indirect. The Phase 2 and pilot studies in the pack used **vinorelbine**, a different vinca analog, not vinblastine. Direct vinblastine use appears only in case reports of multi-drug combinations. In those combinations the contribution of vinblastine cannot be separated from the other drugs.

The pack contains no mechanism-of-action record from DrugBank, so the mechanism above comes from the repurposing rationale in the pack.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high (99.86%) and the mechanism is plausible, but there are no registered trials, and no direct vinblastine clinical evidence beyond combination-therapy case reports. The vinorelbine studies do not transfer to vinblastine. The Health Canada safety information has also not been retrieved.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert (warnings, contraindications) and approved indication text for both DINs
- Confirmed mechanism of action from DrugBank
- Vinblastine-specific clinical data in rhabdomyosarcoma, for example from pediatric sarcoma protocol registries or a dedicated trial
- Review of other predicted indications: **neuroblastoma** has a completed Phase 2 metronomic study (NCT02641314, 18 patients) and a pediatric Phase 1 of vinblastine plus sirolimus (PMID 23956145), which is stronger than the rhabdomyosarcoma evidence. Its evidence level is L2.
