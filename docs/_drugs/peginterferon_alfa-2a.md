# Peginterferon Alfa-2a: From Chronic Hepatitis C to Hepatitis B Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Peginterferon alfa-2a (DrugBank DB00008) is a pegylated interferon historically used to treat chronic viral hepatitis, most notably chronic hepatitis C. The TxGNN model predicts it may also be effective for **Hepatitis B Virus Infection**, a use already supported by **50 clinical trials** and **20 publications** — including several completed Phase 3 RCTs — even though the drug currently has no marketing authorization on file in Canada.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for this candidate is currently a data gap (DG002). Based on the evidence available, peginterferon alfa-2a is a pegylated form of interferon alfa-2a. Its accepted pharmacological mechanism is activation of the JAK-STAT pathway, which induces interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs), suppresses viral replication, and enhances host immune clearance of infected hepatocytes — a mechanism that is broad-spectrum across hepatotropic viruses rather than specific to any single one.

Chronic hepatitis C and chronic hepatitis B are both viral hepatitides that damage the liver through chronic viral replication and immune-mediated inflammation. Because peginterferon alfa-2a's antiviral effect operates through general innate-immune activation rather than a virus-specific target, the same mechanism that supports its use in hepatitis C is mechanistically applicable to hepatitis B.

Notably, the evidence pack's own repurposing rationale indicates that peginterferon alfa-2a (marketed internationally as Pegasys) is **already an approved treatment for HBeAg-positive and HBeAg-negative chronic hepatitis B in multiple countries**. This means the TxGNN prediction is reinforcing an internationally well-established indication rather than proposing a novel, untested use — the main outstanding gap is that this indication and its labeling are not currently documented for the Canadian market in this evidence pack (0 DINs, "Not Marketed").

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The predicted hepatitis B indication is backed by L1-level evidence — multiple completed Phase 3 RCTs (e.g., NCT01369212, NCT01095835, NCT02263079) plus a NEJM-published pivotal trial — and the repurposing rationale confirms peginterferon alfa-2a is already an approved chronic hepatitis B treatment in multiple countries. However, the drug is not currently marketed in Canada, and two data gaps block a full safety review.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada product monograph / warnings and contraindications (currently blocking, DG001)
- Detailed mechanism-of-action documentation from DrugBank (DG002)
- A Canadian regulatory pathway assessment (e.g., Special Access Programme or new drug submission), since no DIN currently exists
- Confirmation of drug-drug interaction data prior to clinical use
