## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the supplied data. Gilteritinib is generally known as a FLT3/AXL tyrosine kinase inhibitor, and this description comes from general knowledge rather than the Evidence Pack.

The prediction rests only on the high knowledge-graph score, and no credible mechanistic link was identified. Bulbar polio is an acute poliovirus infection of motor neurons. Poliovirus enters cells through the CD155 receptor, not through FLT3 or AXL. AXL helps some other viruses enter cells (for example Zika and Ebola), but this has not been shown for poliovirus, so any link is speculative.

The drug's known risks argue against use in an acute infectious disease. These include myelosuppression, differentiation syndrome, QT prolongation and hepatotoxicity. Polio is also prevented by vaccination, and no antiviral treatment is established. The prediction should be treated as a computational hypothesis only.
