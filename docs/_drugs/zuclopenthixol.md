## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Zuclopenthixol is generally described as a dopamine D1/D2 antagonist with additional 5-HT2 and alpha-1 receptor activity, consistent with its use as an antipsychotic. This pharmacology has no known link to inherited retinal degeneration.

The high TxGNN score (99.99%, rank 219) comes from the knowledge graph only. Neither the original indication nor the receptor profile explains why the drug would help a hereditary retinal disorder, and no clinical or preclinical study supports it.

The other eight predictions for this drug have the same weakness. They are:
- Hydranencephaly
- A congenital disorder of glycosylation with defective fucosylation
- Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 1G
- Three myopia-related conditions
- A polymicrogyria syndrome
- Atypical glycine encephalopathy

The three myopia predictions have only a speculative, indirect link through retinal dopamine signalling. Every prediction is at evidence level L5 with a Hold recommendation.
