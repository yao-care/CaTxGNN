## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank is not available. Letrozole is a nonsteroidal aromatase inhibitor. It lowers estrogen synthesis and so removes the growth stimulus for hormone-dependent breast tumours.

Breast cancer is the established labeled use of letrozole, so the predicted indication largely restates an approved use. The high TxGNN score is consistent with this, and the trial and literature base is large.

Most of the evidence involves hormone receptor-positive disease. Letrozole is used as monotherapy (adjuvant, neoadjuvant and metastatic settings) and as the endocrine backbone alongside CDK4/6 inhibitors.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Multiple completed Phase 3 trials, including a large adjuvant trial of letrozole vs tamoxifen (n=8028), support letrozole in breast cancer, which gives Evidence Level L1. Because this is an already-established use, the finding confirms the indication and is not a new repurposing discovery.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert warnings and contraindications (currently a blocking gap)
- Approved-indication text for the Canadian DINs, to verify the label against the predicted indication
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- A use restriction to postmenopausal women or those with ovarian suppression, confirmed receptor status, and a bone density monitoring plan

Lower-ranked predictions in the same pack (ER-negative breast cancer, Ehrlich tumor, breast fibrocystic disease, benign mammary dysplasia) are not supported by direct evidence and should stay on hold.
