## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank is not available (flagged as a High-severity data gap, DG002). Based on information embedded in the evidence pack, selumetinib is described consistently as a **MEK1/2 inhibitor acting on the RAS-MAPK pathway**. Its established use targets NF1-associated plexiform neurofibroma, a tumor driven by RAS-MAPK pathway dysregulation.

Peripheral nerve schwannomas — particularly those associated with Neurofibromatosis Type 2 (NF2) — are driven primarily by loss of the *NF2* tumor suppressor gene (merlin), not by direct RAS mutation. However, downstream of merlin loss, **ERK1/2 (a core MAPK pathway effector) becomes constitutively activated**, converging on the same pathway that selumetinib is designed to block. This convergence is the mechanistic bridge supporting repurposing, even though the upstream genetic drivers differ from NF1.

Importantly, this is not a purely theoretical link: a foundational preclinical study (PMID 19804833) tested selumetinib's predecessor compound (AZD6244) directly on primary human schwannoma cells and demonstrated inhibition of ERK1/2 activation and proliferation. This was later followed by a dedicated Phase 2 clinical trial in NF2 patients and at least one published case report of clinical response, making this the best-supported candidate among the ten indications TxGNN generated for this drug.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails** (for Peripheral Nerve Schwannoma / NF2-related tumors)

**Rationale:**
A dedicated Phase 2 trial and a mechanistically direct preclinical study (selumetinib/AZD6244 tested on primary human schwannoma cells) support biological plausibility and early clinical signal, but the pivotal trial was terminated early with only 10 patients enrolled — evidence is directional, not confirmatory.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Reason for termination of NCT03095248 (efficacy/safety vs. recruitment failure) — critical for interpreting the strength of this evidence
- TFDA/Health Canada label warnings and contraindications (DG001, Blocking) — must be resolved before any S1 safety pre-assessment
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank (DG002, High priority) — needed to formally confirm the pathway-convergence rationale
- Any additional NF2-specific efficacy data (tumor response rate, hearing outcome data) beyond the single case report identified

**For all other 9 candidate indications:** insufficient evidence exists to proceed; continued monitoring for new trials or literature is recommended before any further action.
