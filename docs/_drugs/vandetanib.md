## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Vandetanib blocks VEGFR2, RET and EGFR. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description relies on the target profile reported in the evidence review.

Clear cell renal cell carcinoma is typically driven by loss of the VHL gene. This leads to HIF and VEGF activation and a tumour that depends heavily on new blood vessel growth. Blocking VEGFR2 is therefore biologically plausible. Other VEGFR-targeting kinase inhibitors are already used in kidney cancer, as noted in the reviews listed below.

The clinical evidence is thin. A completed Phase 2 trial exists in von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) disease with renal tumours, and that is the main reason for the L3 rating. The other renal cancer trials were terminated early with very few patients, and no efficacy results were available to us.

## Safety Considerations

- **Class-level safety signals (from literature):** Meta-analyses of VEGFR-targeting kinase inhibitors report hepatic toxicity (PMID 23981115), proteinuria (PMID 32105149) and treatment-related mortality (PMID 22651902). Photosensitivity is also described for this drug class (PMID 30519172). QT prolongation is a recognised concern for vandetanib in the evidence reviewed.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified, and 20 are shown in the data compiled for this report. The following are rated **Major**:
  - Clarithromycin
  - Levofloxacin
  - Dolasetron
  - Palonosetron
  - Picosulfuric acid
  - Polyethylene glycol 3350 with electrolytes
  - Sodium sulfate

  Several Moderate interactions are also listed, including metformin, famotidine, loperamide, dexamethasone, bisacodyl and lactitol. The listed Minor interactions include several proton pump inhibitors and H2 blockers.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the package insert for them.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence level is L3. Only one directly relevant trial was completed (Phase 2, 37 patients with VHL-associated renal tumours), and no results were available. The other renal cancer trials were terminated after 3 and 7 patients. Meanwhile, the drug carries known QT, liver and other class-related safety concerns, and many Major interactions.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Published results of NCT00566995 (response rate, progression-free survival, safety)
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Health Canada
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- A comparison of vandetanib with kinase inhibitors already established in kidney cancer
- An ECG/QT, liver function and urine protein monitoring plan, plus a review of co-medications for Major interactions
