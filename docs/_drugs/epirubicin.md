## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Epirubicin belongs to the anthracycline class (a topoisomerase II inhibitor and DNA intercalator), and drugs of this class are used in some lymphoma regimens, such as CEOP and VEBEP. This gives a plausible biological link to lymphoid malignancies, including primary pulmonary lymphoma.

The link is indirect, however. The modern standard for diffuse large B-cell lymphoma is built on doxorubicin (R-CHOP, Pola-R-CHP), not epirubicin, and epirubicin has not been specifically evaluated in primary pulmonary lymphoma. The high model score should therefore be treated as a hypothesis, not as evidence of benefit.
