## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Certolizumab pegol is a polyethylene-glycolylated antigen-binding fragment of a recombinant human monoclonal antibody. It binds to and neutralizes tumour necrosis factor alpha (TNF-α), a key driver of inflammation in immune-mediated joint and spinal disease.

The link to this prediction is weak. Hypermobility of the coccyx is mainly a mechanical or structural condition, and no clear TNF-driven inflammatory mechanism has been identified for it. The retrieved data contain no trials or publications that study this condition with certolizumab pegol or with other TNF inhibitors.

The high model score therefore remains a computational signal only. It would need independent biological or clinical support before it could be treated as a credible repurposing lead.
