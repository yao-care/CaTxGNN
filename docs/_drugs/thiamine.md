## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Thiamine is known as a cofactor for carbohydrate-metabolism enzymes such as pyruvate dehydrogenase and transketolase, but this report cannot confirm the original indication from the licence records.

No mechanistic link to thyroid hormone receptor beta signalling is supported by the data compiled for this report. The condition is caused by a receptor mutation, and nothing indicates that thiamine's metabolic role affects that defect. The high score (0.9943) is a model output only and should be treated as a hypothesis, not as evidence of benefit.

For context, the same data list two other predictions, both with no supporting trials: primary hereditary glaucoma (0.9940, L5) and open-angle glaucoma (0.9936). The open-angle glaucoma entry has only indirect observational and exploratory literature, mainly nutrient-status associations. It does not establish treatment benefit and is not analysed further here.
