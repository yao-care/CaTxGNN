## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Flupentixol is generally known as a dopamine D1/D2 antagonist used as an antipsychotic. No mechanism connects this activity to inherited retinal dystrophy.

The 99.99% score comes from graph-based prediction alone. The score does not show that flupentixol acts on the disease biology, and no pathway linking the original use to the predicted indication has been established.

The literature retrieved for this prediction covers ocular and orbital developmental conditions, and none of it involves flupentixol. The prediction should therefore be treated as a hypothesis-generating signal, not a candidate with supporting evidence.
