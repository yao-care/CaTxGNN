## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Tizanidine is known as a central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. By reducing the release of excitatory neurotransmitters, it is thought to dampen trigeminal pain transmission and central sensitization, which are plausible targets for migraine prevention.

Reviews of headache prophylaxis discuss tizanidine alongside other agents used for chronic migraine and chronic daily headache. Its effect on muscle tone and tension is also relevant to headache disorders with a muscular component.

Caveats: the best randomized evidence (PMID 12167135, 2002) studied chronic daily headache, a group that includes chronic migraine, migrainous headache and tension-type headache, rather than migraine alone. The Phase 3 migraine trial has no verified results.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Major-level examples include famotidine, cimetidine, morphine, dolasetron and obeticholic acid. Moderate-level examples include hyoscyamine, loperamide, atropine, clarithromycin and levofloxacin. Known risks to manage include sedation, hypotension, liver toxicity and CYP1A2 inhibitors such as fluvoxamine and ciprofloxacin.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
