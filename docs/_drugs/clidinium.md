## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Clidinium is known as a quaternary antimuscarinic, which blocks muscarinic receptors and reduces smooth-muscle contraction and secretions.

A theoretical link to cauda equina syndrome would run through reduced detrusor overactivity in bladder dysfunction that follows nerve-root involvement. This link is speculative. The condition commonly involves urinary retention, and an anticholinergic could make that worse, so the prediction may be unsafe rather than helpful.

The score is high, but a score alone is not clinical evidence. No trial or publication supports this use.
