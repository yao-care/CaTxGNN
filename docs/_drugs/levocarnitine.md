## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Levocarnitine is the biologically active form of carnitine. Carnitine is generally known to carry long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for energy production.

No plausible mechanistic link was found between levocarnitine and this syndrome. The syndrome is an ultra-rare disorder of vascular and connective-tissue origin, and carnitine-dependent fatty acid transport has no established role in it.
