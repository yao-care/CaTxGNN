## One-Sentence Summary

Fluphenazine is a high-potency phenothiazine antipsychotic that blocks D2 dopamine receptors.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **retinal dystrophy with or without extraocular anomalies**, but the prediction rests on knowledge-graph proximity alone, with **0 clinical trials** and **16 publications** that do not mention fluphenazine.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Fluphenazine is a phenothiazine D2 dopamine receptor antagonist. Nothing links this mechanism to the pathways behind inherited retinal degeneration.

The very high TxGNN score (0.9999) appears to reflect proximity in the knowledge graph, not pharmacology. The 16 retrieved papers cover general ophthalmology topics such as diplopia, ptosis, orbital disease and ophthalmoplegia. None mentions fluphenazine.

Phenothiazines are also associated with retinal toxicity at high doses, which argues against benefit. Overall, this prediction is not mechanistically supported.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by a model score, with no clinical trials, no drug-specific literature and no plausible mechanism. Known retinal toxicity of phenothiazines points the other way. The other top-ranked predictions (syndromic myopia, X-linked myopia, hydranencephaly, glycosylation and metabolic disorders, and others) are also L5 with no supporting evidence.

The only prediction with real supporting literature is **manic bipolar affective disorder** (rank 10, L3). It rests on case reports and on retrospective or consensus data for long-acting injectable antipsychotics in bipolar disorder. It has no registered trials and no Phase 2/3 RCT evidence for fluphenazine itself. It is better framed as a research question, subject to review of extrapyramidal effects and the risk of depressive switching.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA) and any preclinical rationale for a retinal effect
- Package insert warnings and contraindications for the Canadian products
- Approved indication text for the three Canadian licenses
- A retinal safety assessment, given phenothiazine retinal toxicity
- If pursuing the bipolar mania direction, a dedicated evidence review of fluphenazine-specific data and safety
