## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Amlodipine belongs to the calcium channel blocker class. It is widely used to lower blood pressure, and its efficacy in that setting is well established. Mechanistically, it may be applicable to brain stem infarction.

A plausible link is vascular protection through blood pressure lowering and calcium channel blockade. Blood pressure control matters for stroke prevention. However, this link is a hypothesis only. No trial or publication in the Evidence Pack tests amlodipine in brain stem infarction, and the prediction rests on the model score alone.
