# Moclobemide: From Depression to Agoraphobia

## One-Sentence Summary

Moclobemide is a reversible inhibitor of monoamine oxidase A (RIMA), marketed in Canada under the brand name Maneris. The literature describes it as an antidepressant, but the supplied Canadian licence records list no approved indication text.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **agoraphobia**, and there are **0 registered clinical trials** and **12 publications** on the topic, including **2 randomized controlled trials** in panic disorder with or without agoraphobia.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the supplied record. Based on general RIMA pharmacology, moclobemide inhibits monoamine oxidase A, which raises serotonin, noradrenaline and dopamine. These are the same monoaminergic pathways targeted by antidepressants used in panic disorder with agoraphobia and social anxiety disorder.

Depression and anxiety disorders often share treatments. Older reviews report that MAO inhibitors are effective in controlled studies of panic disorder with agoraphobia and social phobia. Moclobemide is described as better tolerated than older irreversible MAOIs, with fewer dietary restrictions and drug interactions.

The literature mostly covers panic disorder and social anxiety disorder, not agoraphobia alone. The link to agoraphobia is therefore partly indirect, because agoraphobia is usually studied as part of panic disorder.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Published RCTs in panic disorder with agoraphobia and consistent reviews support the biological plausibility of the link, but there are no registered trials specific to agoraphobia. The Canadian package insert warnings and contraindications are missing, which blocks safety screening. Until that gap is closed, this remains a research question.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert warnings and contraindications (blocking)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Full-text review of the two RCTs (PMIDs 10448444 and 10361962) to extract efficacy results specific to agoraphobia
- Confirmation of the approved indication text for the 5 DINs
