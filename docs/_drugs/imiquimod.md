## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for imiquimod is not populated in this evidence pack's drug record (data gap DG002). However, the per-indication repurposing rationale supplies the mechanistic basis: imiquimod is a TLR7 agonist that locally induces cytokines (IFN-α, TNF-α, IL-12) to activate innate and cell-mediated immunity, driving clearance of HPV-related or UV-damage-induced dysplastic/hyperproliferative epithelium.

This mechanism is a direct extension of imiquimod's already-established use in dysplastic skin lesions. "Pre-malignant neoplasm" as a disease category encompasses conditions such as actinic keratosis, cervical/vulvar/anal intraepithelial neoplasia (CIN/VIN/AIN), lentigo maligna, and Bowenoid papulosis — all of which share the same underlying biology (HPV infection or chronic UV-induced epithelial dysplasia) that imiquimod's immune-activation mechanism is designed to target.

Because the predicted indication is mechanistically and histologically adjacent to imiquimod's known clinical use rather than a novel target, the TxGNN prediction is biologically plausible and is further corroborated by a substantial body of directly relevant clinical and literature evidence across multiple pre-malignant lesion types (skin, cervix, vulva, oral mucosa).
