## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Levodopa's known pharmacological mechanism is to serve as a dopamine precursor, restoring central dopaminergic tone in movement disorders such as Parkinson's disease. Rasmussen subacute encephalitis, by contrast, is a rare, chronic inflammatory condition affecting one cerebral hemisphere, driven by T-cell–mediated autoimmune/inflammatory activity and refractory epilepsy; standard treatments are immunomodulatory (IVIG, corticosteroids, plasma exchange) or hemispherectomy.

There is no established pharmacological or pathophysiological link between dopamine repletion and neuroinflammatory/autoimmune disease processes. Detailed original indication and mechanism-of-action data for levodopa are also not available in this evidence pack, further limiting any mechanistic argument.
