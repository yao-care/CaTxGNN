## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Decitabine is generally known as a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor (hypomethylating agent), a class used in myeloid malignancies including acute myeloid leukaemia and myelodysplastic syndromes.

Refractory cytopenia of childhood is the most common paediatric MDS subtype. Aberrant DNA methylation and clonal myeloid dysplasia are shared biology in MDS, which gives a plausible mechanistic link. This rationale rests on the general pharmacology of the drug class, not on mechanism data supplied here.

The high TxGNN score (0.9903) is a computational output only and is not clinical evidence.
