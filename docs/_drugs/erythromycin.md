## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Erythromycin is a macrolide antibiotic that inhibits the bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit. Macrolides also have anti-inflammatory (immunomodulatory) properties.

Mechanistically, this may matter for blepharitis-associated ocular surface inflammation, which can accompany keratoconjunctivitis. The supplied data does not show a direct link between erythromycin and punctate epithelial keratoconjunctivitis. The very high TxGNN score (0.999) is a model prediction only and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.
