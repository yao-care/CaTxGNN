## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source records. Methylprednisolone is a glucocorticoid, and its broad immunosuppressive activity is mediated through the glucocorticoid receptor.

Alopecia areata is a T-cell-mediated autoimmune attack on the anagen hair follicle, which loses its immune privilege. High-dose methylprednisolone, given orally or as an IV pulse, suppresses this immune activity. This is the mechanistic basis for the prediction.

Its use is limited to severe or refractory disease. Hair loss often relapses after the steroid is withdrawn, and cumulative steroid toxicity is a concern. Universalis disease is typically refractory to pulse steroids and relapses afterwards.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information. No warnings or contraindications were captured, and no drug interaction records were found.

Literature on this indication points to two limits. Relapse is common after steroid withdrawal, and cumulative steroid toxicity restricts use to severe or refractory disease.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The mechanistic rationale is sound, and one completed Phase 4 trial plus several clinical series support use in severe, refractory alopecia areata. However, the evidence is small-scale and largely non-randomized, with no Phase 3 RCT, and relapse after withdrawal is common.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert warnings and contraindications. This is a blocking gap for safety screening.
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank.
- Results and design details (randomization, control arm, relapse follow-up) for NCT01167946.
- Approved indication text for the Canadian licences, to confirm the original indication.
- A safety and relapse monitoring plan for repeated or long-term pulse use.

The other predicted indications are much weaker. Idiopathic steroid-sensitive nephrotic syndrome has L3 evidence and is a research question, while the rest are model predictions only and should be held.
