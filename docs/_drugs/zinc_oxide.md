# Zinc Oxide: From Skin Conditions to Acne

## One-Sentence Summary

Zinc oxide is a topical skin protectant originally used for conditions such as dermatitis, fungal skin infections, diaper-area irritation, hemorrhoids and burns.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **acne**, but there are **no registered clinical trials** and only **3 publications** (a review, a preclinical study and a small clinical study), so this remains a research question.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Based on known information, zinc oxide is a topical zinc preparation whose use in several inflammatory and irritated skin conditions is well established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to acne.

Zinc is generally thought to have antibacterial effects, including against *Cutibacterium acnes*, partly through zinc ion release and reactive oxygen species generation. It also has mild anti-inflammatory, sebum-regulating and astringent properties. Its existing use in dermatitis and other skin conditions suggests it is tolerated on the skin.

This link rests on general zinc biology, not on a documented drug-specific mechanism. The very high TxGNN score is a computational prediction only and does not replace clinical evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The acne prediction has a high model score but no registered clinical trials, and the only supporting literature is a review, a preclinical study and a small clinical study. The mechanistic link relies on general zinc biology. The other four predictions (anorectal stricture, anal polyp, papillary conjunctivitis, postinfectious vasculitis) have no supporting evidence at all and are weaker still.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Health Canada, so safety screening can proceed
- Mechanism of action data for zinc oxide (for example, from DrugBank)
- Confirmation of whether the 2004 split-face study actually tested zinc oxide
- Controlled clinical studies of topical zinc oxide in acne, with a defined formulation and concentration
