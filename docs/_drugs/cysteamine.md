## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available in the record. Based on known information, cysteamine converts lysosomal cystine into mixed disulfides that exit the lysosome through an alternative transporter. It is also known for antioxidant and glutathione-supporting activity. Its efficacy in cystinosis is established.

A glutathione or oxidative-stress rationale for mitochondrial disease is conceivable, but it is speculative. No trials or literature were retrieved to support the link between cysteamine and this disorder. The high score most likely reflects associations in the knowledge graph, not demonstrated therapeutic benefit.
