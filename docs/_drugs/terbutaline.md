## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Terbutaline belongs to the β2-adrenergic agonist class, which relaxes airway smooth muscle and relieves reversible bronchospasm.

Structural airway anomalies are often accompanied by bronchospasm or airway hyperreactivity, so a β2 agonist could ease those symptoms. It would not correct the malformation itself. The high score most likely reflects the model's association with airway disease in general rather than disease-specific efficacy. Some of the retrieved literature concerns the differential diagnosis of airway stenosis in children, which is a diagnostic question and not a treatment indication.
