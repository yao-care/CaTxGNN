## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Aflibercept acts as a decoy receptor ("trap") that binds VEGF-A, VEGF-B and placental growth factor (PlGF). Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description relies on the drug's known class.

Esotropia is a form of strabismus (inward turning of the eye) driven mainly by neuromuscular and sensorimotor factors. No clear mechanistic route from VEGF/PlGF blockade to esotropia is evident. The high score (0.9938) is a model output only; no trial or publication in the compiled data backs it. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis, not as a supported repurposing opportunity.
