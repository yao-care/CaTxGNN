# Eptinezumab: From Migraine Disorder to Migraine with Brainstem Aura

## One-Sentence Summary

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known class information, eptinezumab is an anti-CGRP monoclonal antibody. Its efficacy in migraine prevention is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to migraine with brainstem aura.

Migraine with brainstem aura is a subtype of migraine, so involvement of the CGRP pathway is biologically plausible. A post hoc analysis of the PROMISE-1 and PROMISE-2 trials looked at eptinezumab in patients with self-reported aura. Case reports of anti-CGRP antibodies in aura also exist. However, none of these specifically address brainstem aura.

There are also reasons for caution. A PACAP38 provocation RCT suggests that PACAP38-induced migraine attacks can occur independently of CGRP signaling, which may limit the benefit of CGRP blockade in some patients. The role of CGRP in cortical spreading depression, which is thought to underlie aura, is not fully established. The very high TxGNN score reflects the close nosological proximity to the approved migraine indication. It is not independent clinical validation.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Two recent observational studies in the literature examined class-level safety signals for CGRP inhibitors: glaucoma risk and cerebrovascular events (ischaemic stroke/TIA). These are worth reviewing in any follow-up safety assessment.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is mechanistically plausible, but it rests on model output and indirect evidence only (Evidence Level L4). No clinical trials target migraine with brainstem aura, and the available data on aura are limited to a post hoc analysis and case reports. The Health Canada package insert warnings and contraindications have not been obtained, so safety screening cannot proceed. The drug should be treated as a research question for now.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada package insert (warnings, contraindications, approved indication text) for both DINs
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Subtype-specific evidence, such as a subgroup analysis of existing eptinezumab trials in brainstem aura or a dedicated pilot study
- Review of the PACAP-related evidence on whether CGRP blockade alone is sufficient for this subtype
- A safety review covering the cerebrovascular and glaucoma signals reported for the CGRP inhibitor class
