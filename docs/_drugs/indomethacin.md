## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Indomethacin is a non-selective COX-1/COX-2 inhibitor that reduces prostaglandin-mediated inflammation, pain and fever.

Brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome is a congenital limb malformation of genetic developmental origin. COX inhibition does not act on the underlying pathway, so there is no plausible mechanistic link between the drug and this condition. The very high TxGNN score (0.9997) is a graph-based prediction only, and it is not backed by any trial or publication.

**A better-supported candidate in the same pack:** juvenile idiopathic arthritis (rank 8, score 99.84%, evidence level L3). NSAIDs are a long-standing symptomatic therapy for it. One double-blind crossover study (PMID 362571, 1978, 30 children) compared ketoprofen with indomethacin, and indomethacin emerged as the preferred drug. This may be an existing or off-label use rather than true repurposing, so it should be checked against labeling. The evidence is old and symptomatic only, and it does not show disease modification. It is not the focus of this report.
