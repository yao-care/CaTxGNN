## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Valsartan belongs to the angiotensin receptor blocker class, which blocks the AT1 receptor of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS). Mechanistically, RAAS blockade lowers blood pressure and may protect the kidney in hypertensive kidney injury, so the prediction is biologically plausible.

However, this link is a hypothesis only. The one publication attached to this prediction (PMID 24368192) tested avosentan, an endothelin receptor antagonist, in a rat model of hypertensive nephropathy. It did not study valsartan or malignant hypertensive renal disease in patients, so it offers only indirect support. No clinical trials are registered.
