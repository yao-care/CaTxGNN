## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the evidence pack (Data Gap DG002). Based on public information, tenofovir alafenamide is a prodrug of tenofovir, a nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitor that blocks retroviral reverse transcription — the same mechanism class used against HIV-1.

Simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) is the macaque-infecting relative of HIV, and it is the standard non-human primate model used to study HIV pathogenesis, prevention, and treatment. Mechanistically, a reverse transcriptase inhibitor effective against HIV-1 would be expected to show antiviral activity against SIV/SHIV as well, since both viruses share the same replication machinery. This explains why nearly all supporting evidence comes from macaque PrEP and treatment studies rather than a genuinely new disease target.

It is important to note, however, that "simian immunodeficiency virus infection" is an animal-model research indication, not a human clinical disease that would be pursued as a marketed indication in Canada. The prediction is therefore best interpreted as confirming TAF's known antiretroviral class activity rather than identifying a novel therapeutic opportunity.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
TAF is not marketed in Canada (0 DINs), a blocking safety data gap (DG001) prevents initial safety assessment, and the supporting evidence for the predicted indication is almost entirely preclinical macaque-model research rather than completed human trials — insufficient to proceed even with guardrails.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada product monograph warnings/contraindications (resolves DG001, Blocking)
- Confirmed mechanism of action and original approved indication from DrugBank (resolves DG002)
- Clarification of clinical translational relevance, since "simian immunodeficiency virus infection" is an animal research model rather than a human indication pursued for Canadian market authorization
- Monitoring of NCT03577782 for completion and confirmation of TAF's specific role in the trial regimen
