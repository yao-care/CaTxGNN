## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Oxybutynin is an antimuscarinic and antispasmodic agent. Its efficacy in its approved uses is established, but no mechanistic link to restless legs syndrome (RLS) has been identified.

The link is weak. RLS is mainly associated with dopaminergic dysfunction and brain iron deficiency. Anticholinergic agents have not been shown to benefit RLS, and some sedating anticholinergics may worsen symptoms. The high TxGNN score (0.997) is a graph-based prediction with no clinical data behind it, so it should be treated as a hypothesis only.
