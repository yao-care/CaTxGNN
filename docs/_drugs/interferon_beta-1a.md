## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, interferon beta-1a is an immunomodulatory biologic. Its efficacy in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis is established, but a mechanistic link to the predicted indication has not been identified.

Jeune syndrome is a ciliopathy, a skeletal dysplasia with situs abnormalities. Interferon beta-1a acts on the immune system, and no plausible pathway connects it to ciliary function or skeletal development. The high score most likely reflects the structure of the knowledge graph rather than biological relevance.

The other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern. Most are congenital or chromosomal structural conditions (Pierre Robin syndrome, orofacial clefting, chromosome deletions, Laubry-Pezzi syndrome). An immunomodulator is not expected to alter these. For two ovarian tumour candidates (rete ovarii cystadenoma and borderline ovarian serous tumour), only a weak, theoretical link exists through the antiproliferative effects of interferons. No clinical evidence supports it.
