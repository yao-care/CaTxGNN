# Irinotecan: From Established Chemotherapy Use to Female Breast Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Irinotecan is a topoisomerase I-inhibiting chemotherapy drug that is marketed in Canada, but the available data do not list its approved indications.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**.
The pack lists **22 clinical trials** and **20 publications**, but only about 3 trials test irinotecan directly in breast cancer, and most of the literature concerns sacituzumab govitecan, a different SN-38 product.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Irinotecan is a prodrug. Carboxylesterases convert it to SN-38, which inhibits topoisomerase I. This causes DNA damage and apoptosis in rapidly dividing tumour cells. The mechanism is not specific to one tumour type, so activity in breast carcinoma is biologically plausible. Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank were not available in the pack, so this description comes from the evidence summary.

The strongest indirect support comes from sacituzumab govitecan, an anti-Trop-2 antibody conjugated to SN-38. It has shown clinical benefit in metastatic triple-negative and HR+/HER2- breast cancer. This supports SN-38 as an active payload in breast cancer. However, it is a different product and does not establish the efficacy or dosing of irinotecan itself.

The TxGNN score is a computational prediction only. The original indications were not available, so the link between the original and new indication could not be checked.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The mechanism is plausible, and a few Phase 1/2 studies test irinotecan directly in breast cancer. However, most supporting evidence is indirect (sacituzumab govitecan, liposomal formulations, mixed solid-tumour studies). Safety data are also missing, so the drug cannot yet pass safety screening.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Health Canada product monograph warnings and contraindications, which are the blocking gap
- Approved indications and dosage forms for the Canadian DINs
- Results from NCT00072852 and NCT03562390, the two Phase 2 irinotecan trials in metastatic breast cancer
- Results from the PHENOMENAL study (PMID 41371050) to check the liposomal irinotecan signal
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
