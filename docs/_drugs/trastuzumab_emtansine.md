# Trastuzumab emtansine: From HER2-Positive Breast Carcinoma to Progesterone-Receptor Positive Breast Cancer

## One-Sentence Summary

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) is an antibody-drug conjugate originally used to treat HER2-positive breast carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **progesterone-receptor positive breast cancer**, but this is largely a subtype of the already approved population (HR+/HER2+), and the supporting evidence consists of **0 clinical trials** and **15 publications** (mostly reviews, guidelines and case reports).

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, T-DM1 links the anti-HER2 antibody trastuzumab to the cytotoxic agent DM1. It binds HER2 on tumour cells and delivers DM1 into them.

PR-positive breast cancer overlaps heavily with HR+/HER2+ disease, which is part of the approved HER2-positive population. Activity therefore depends on HER2 status, not PR status. This is best seen as a subtype of the existing indication rather than true repurposing. Patients who are PR-positive but HER2-negative would not be expected to benefit.

The same pattern appears in other breast cancer predictions. For PR-negative disease, luminal B HER2-positive tumours and HER2-positive subtypes in general, the rationale is the same HER2-directed mechanism. Predictions for other tumour types (for example synovial, tenosynovial giant cell, HHV-8-related tumours and ectomesenchymoma) have no clinical or literature support and no plausible HER2 link. They most likely reflect knowledge-graph associations.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The PR-positive prediction has a very high model score, but no registered trials. The literature is limited to reviews, guidelines and case reports. Because efficacy depends on HER2 status, this is a subtype of the existing HER2-positive indication, not a new one. Other predicted indications have no supporting evidence and no plausible mechanism.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Health Canada
- Mechanism of action data
- Subgroup analyses of existing T-DM1 trials by hormone receptor status (HR+/HER2+) to confirm benefit in PR-positive patients
- Confirmation that the PR-positive population is already covered by the current approved HER2-positive indication, which would make this a labelling question rather than repurposing
