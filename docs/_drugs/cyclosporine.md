## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. From general pharmacology, cyclosporine is a calcineurin inhibitor that blocks NFAT-dependent T-cell activation.

In chronic granulomatous disease (CGD), the immune system shows dysregulated, excessive inflammation, so suppressing T-cell activity could theoretically dampen this inflammation. This remains a hypothesis only.

The only linked publication reports outcomes of stem cell transplantation in CGD. Calcineurin inhibitors are likely used there to prevent graft-versus-host disease, but this gives context only and does not show that cyclosporine treats CGD. The high score is a model prediction without clinical validation. Because no cited study directly examines cyclosporine in this disease, the evidence level is L5.
